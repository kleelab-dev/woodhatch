What can WoodHatch build?

Kitchen cabinets, TV consoles, wardrobes, bed frames, shoe racks, dining sets, office and working tables, and chairs, couches and sofas. We also do epoxy art, revamping and full interior decor.

Will I see the design before you build?

Yes. Every project is modeled in 3D with a curated material list for your approval before anything is cut.

How long does a project take?

Standard collection pieces take 2 to 4 weeks. Custom projects take 4 to 8 weeks. Full turnkey timelines depend on the size of the project.

Do you install?

Yes. Our team handles professional installation across Lagos and Abuja.

Can you redo furniture or a room I already have?

Yes. Revamping is one of our services. Send photos of the space and we will suggest what to keep, what to rebuild and what to restyle.

How much does it cost?

Every piece is made to measure, so we quote per project after the consultation. Tell us what you need and we will come back with a price.
